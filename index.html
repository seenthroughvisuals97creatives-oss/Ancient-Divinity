The story

There are things we experience that we were never taught how to speak about.

Sometimes the unfamiliar is treated as something to fear, when what it may need first is space, reassurance, and understanding.

This is a place where something unusual can exist without being attacked for being unusual.

A place to breathe. To express. To listen. To question. To discover.

Not everything has to be placed into a box before it is allowed to exist.

And perhaps the unusual is not new at all. Perhaps it is ancient.

The words move gently. You decide whether they continue.
